How Remote Assistance Works

Three simple steps to get help from your family, no matter where they are

Open Remote Help

Tap the Remote Assistance button. A unique 6-digit code appears on your screen.

2

Share the Code

3

They Guide You

Your child sees your screen and can draw arrows and circles to show you where to tap.

Your Privacy Is Protected

We take security seriously. Here is how we keep you safe

  • Family-Only Access

    Only people you have approved can connect to your screen. Strangers cannot request access under any circumstances.

  • One-Time Codes

    Each session generates a new, unique connection code. Old codes cannot be reused, preventing unauthorized access.

  • You Stay in Control

    You can end the session at any time with one tap. Your child can only view your screen, never control it without permission.

  • No Data Stored

    Screen content is never recorded or saved. Once the session ends, everything is gone. Your privacy is absolute.
