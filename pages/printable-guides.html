Printable Guides

Download and print step-by-step instructions. Take them to the bank, hospital, or anywhere you need them. Large print, simple steps, always ready.

Hospital Visit Checklist

Everything you need for a smooth hospital appointment

  1. Bring your ID card and health insurance card
  2. Arrive 30 minutes before your appointment
  3. Go to the registration desk first
  4. Show your appointment confirmation
  5. Wait in the designated waiting area
  6. Bring a list of current medications
  7. Write down questions for your doctor

Bank Visit Checklist

Step-by-step for common banking tasks

  1. Bring your bank card and ID card
  2. Take a queue number at the entrance
  3. Wait for your number to be called
  4. Tell the teller exactly what you need
  5. Count your money before leaving the counter
  6. Keep your receipt in a safe place
  7. Never share your PIN with anyone

Taking the Train

A complete guide from ticket to destination

  1. Arrive at the station 45 minutes early
  2. Find your train number on the departure board
  3. Go to the correct waiting hall
  4. Check your ticket for carriage and seat number
  5. Line up when your train is announced
  6. Show your ID and ticket at the gate
  7. Find your seat using the numbers above the seats
  8. Keep your ticket until you exit the destination station
