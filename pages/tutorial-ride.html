Using Ride-Hailing Apps

Call a taxi from your phone anytime. No need to stand on the street waiting.

Step
1

Open the Ride App

Find the ride-hailing app on your phone. Common apps include DiDi and other local services. Tap to open.

Step
2

Set Your Pickup Location

The app usually detects your current location automatically. If it is wrong, tap the location box and enter your address manually.

Step
3

Enter Your Destination

Type the address or name of where you want to go. The app will show estimated arrival time and fare.

Step
4

Choose Car Type

Select "Express" for a standard affordable ride, or "Comfort" for a nicer car. Express is usually sufficient for everyday trips.

Step
5

Confirm and Wait

Tap "Confirm" to request a ride. The app will match you with a driver. You can see the driver name, car type, and license plate number.

Step
6

Track Your Ride

Watch the driver approach on the map. When the car arrives, check the license plate matches. Get in and enjoy your ride.

Step
7

Rate After the Trip

After arriving, the app asks you to rate the driver. A 5-star rating is the default. Payment is automatic through the app.
