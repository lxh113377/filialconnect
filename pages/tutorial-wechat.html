WeChat Complete Guide

Master the most useful WeChat features step by step. From chatting with family to paying at stores.

Step
1

Open WeChat

Find the WeChat icon on your phone. It is a green icon with two chat bubbles. Tap it to open.

Step
2

Add Contacts

Tap the "+" button at the top right, then "Add Contacts." You can search by WeChat ID or phone number, or scan a QR code.

Step
3

Send Messages

Tap a contact name to open a chat. Type your message and tap Send. You can also send voice messages by holding the microphone button.

Step
4

Make Video Calls

In a chat, tap the "+" icon and select "Video Call." This lets you see your family face-to-face, even from far away.

Step
5

Use WeChat Pay

Tap "Me" then "Pay" to set up WeChat Pay. You can pay at stores by scanning QR codes, send money to friends, and pay bills.

Step
6

Order Groceries

In WeChat, tap "Discover" then "Mini Programs." Search for grocery delivery apps. You can order fresh food and daily necessities delivered to your door.

Step
7

Stay Safe on WeChat

Do not click unfamiliar links. Do not transfer money to strangers. If someone asks for money through a new number, call your family to verify first.
